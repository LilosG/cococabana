If you're looking to maximize value, here's how to approach it:

**Arrive Between 4–5 PM.** The first hour of happy hour tends to be less crowded than 5–6 PM, especially on weekdays. You'll get faster service and a better pick of seating on the rooftop. The view doesn't change, but the experience does when you're not elbow-to-elbow with the post-work crowd.

**Budget Per Person:** For a casual happy hour visit, expect to spend $15–25 per person if you're ordering two cocktails and one or two small plates to share. **Group Dynamics Matter:** Happy hour is perfect for groups because small plates are designed to share. Four people ordering three cocktails each and three small plates to split works out to roughly $18–22 per person, including tip. That's efficient spending for a rooftop experience.
