## Venue Rental: The Base Cost

The first line item is venue rental. At Cococabana, private event space rental starts at **$500** and scales with your guest count and timing.

**What you get:**
- Exclusive or semi-private rooftop access (depending on party size)

- Standard setup (linens, glassware, basic décor)

**Timing matters.** Friday and Saturday evenings command higher rates than weekday or afternoon slots. A Saturday night party for 40 people might run $750–$1,000 just for the space. A Thursday afternoon gathering of 25 could be $500–$650.

Most venues in Oceanside charge $300–$800 for comparable rooftop space. Cococabana's rates sit in the middle—you're paying for the pier view, the Caribbean vibe, and the fact that the space is actually designed for events, not just squeezed in around regular bar service.

**Pro tip:** The venue fee often applies as a credit toward your final food and beverage bill if you spend above a certain threshold (usually $1,500–$2,000 depending on party size). Ask about this during planning; it can effectively reduce your true out-of-pocket venue cost.

Food is where most people underestimate their budget. Cococabana offers two approaches: plated meals or passed appetizers and shareable plates.

**Plated Entrée Approach (Most Common for Sit-Down Parties)**

Per-person pricing: **$28–$48**

This includes a protein, sides, and a starter or dessert. Examples:
- Jerk chicken with rice and beans: $28–$32
- Grilled fish with seasonal vegetables: $35–$42

A 40-person party with mixed entrées averages $35 per person = $1,400 in food costs alone.

A 60-person cocktail-style party with $25 per-person appetizer spend = $1,500.

## Beverage Costs: The Second Major Line Item

Drinks are where rooftop parties either stay reasonable or spiral. Cococabana offers three beverage service models:

**Open Bar (Full Liquor)**

Cost: **$18–$28 per person per hour**

Guests order whatever they want—cocktails, beer, wine, spirits. The bar tracks consumption, and you pay at the end. For a 3-hour party, 50 guests, and moderate drinking, expect $2,700–$4,200 in bar costs.

This is the most popular choice for birthday celebrations because guests don't have to think about it.

**Signature Cocktail Package**

Cost: **$12–$16 per person per hour**

You pick 2–3 signature drinks (often a rum-based Caribbean cocktail, a lighter option, and a non-alcoholic choice). Guests order from those options only. Beer and wine are typically included. This approach controls costs and adds personality to the party.

Example: A mojito-inspired rum punch, a passion fruit margarita, and a virgin piña colada. For 50 people over 3 hours, budget $1,800–$2,400.

**Limited Bar (Beer, Wine, Soft Drinks)**

Cost: **$8–$12 per person per hour**

If your group doesn't prioritize cocktails, this is the most economical option. You're covering beer, wine, and non-alcoholic beverages only. For a 3-hour event with 50 people, expect $1,200–$1,800.

**Non-Alcoholic Beverages**

Always included: water, coffee, tea, soft drinks, and fresh juices. No separate charge.

**Alcohol Service Notes**

California law requires that all alcohol service follow state regulations. Cococabana staff are trained and certified. If your group includes anyone under 21, the bar team will check ID. No exceptions—this protects everyone.

## Hidden Costs to Avoid

**Tax and Gratuity Are Not Hidden—But They Add Up**

California sales tax is 8.75%. Gratuity (18–20% for parties) is standard and applied to the full bill, including food, drinks, and venue fees. These aren't surprises; they're part of the calculation. If your subtotal is $4,000, expect an additional $1,100–$1,200 in tax and gratuity combined.

**Décor and Florals**

Cococabana includes basic setup. If you want custom centerpieces, balloons, or floral arrangements, that's an add-on. Budget $200–$600 depending on complexity. You can bring your own florist, or Cococabana can recommend trusted vendors.

**Cake or Dessert Service**

If you're bringing an outside cake, there's typically a $1–$2 per-person plating fee (Cococabana staff plates and serves it). If you order dessert from the kitchen, it's included in your food pricing.

**Photography or Videography**

Not a venue cost, but worth budgeting. Professional photographers in Oceanside run $400–$1,200 for 2–4 hours of coverage.

**Parking**

The Brick Hotel has a parking structure. Validated parking for guests is often available; confirm this during planning. It's not a direct party cost, but it affects your guests' experience.

## How to Lock in Your Price and Avoid Surprises

1. **Get a Written Quote.** Don't rely on phone estimates. Ask for a formal quote that includes venue, food, drinks, tax, and gratuity. It should be itemized.

2. **Confirm Your Final Headcount 10 Days Before.** Venues hold estimates for a certain number of guests. If your final count changes, your bill adjusts. Lock it in early to avoid last-minute surprises.

3. **Discuss Bar Minimums.** If you're doing a signature cocktail package or limited bar, ask if there's a minimum spend. Some venues require you to hit a certain dollar amount; if you don't, you pay the difference anyway.

4. **Ask About Timing.** Evening events (after 6 p.m.) and weekend dates cost more. Weekday or afternoon parties are cheaper. If you have flexibility, this is where you save money.

5. **Clarify What's Included in "Setup."** Does the venue rental include tables, chairs, linens, glassware? What about ice, napkins, and basic bar setup? Know exactly what you're getting.

6. **Discuss Cancellation and Rescheduling Policies.** Life happens. Understand the terms if you need to cancel or move your date.

## Timing Your Party to Save Money

**Cheapest Options:**
- Weekday afternoons (Tuesday–Thursday, 2–5 p.m.): Venue fees are lowest, and you avoid peak pricing.
- Off-season (September–October, January–February): Fewer events mean better availability and potential discounts.

**Mid-Range:**
- Friday afternoons or early evening (before 6 p.m.): Reasonable rates, still feels like a celebration.
- Sunday brunch or early evening: Cheaper than Saturday, still weekend vibes.

**Premium (Most Expensive):**
- Saturday nights, especially 6–10 p.m.: Peak demand, peak pricing.
- Holiday weekends: Expect surcharges.

If you're flexible on your date, moving from a Saturday night to a Friday afternoon could save you $1,000–$2,000 on a 50-person party.
